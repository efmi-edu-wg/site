IMIA Recommendations – second revision (2023)

The International Medical Informatics Association (IMIA) recommendations describe the knowledge and skills in BMHI that health professionals, health informaticians and IT specialists need. The second revision adds learning outcomes for new topics and is the main reference for accreditation.

Read the 2023 recommendations (open access)

How to use them

  • Map your course learning outcomes to the recommended domains.
  • Pick the right depth for each audience (users, BMHI specialists, IT specialists).
  • Use the mapping as evidence for accreditation.

EFMI guidance

EFMI supports the IMIA recommendations and, through its Accreditation and Certification committee (AC2), promotes the accreditation of BMHI programmes and the certification of knowledge and skills in Europe.
