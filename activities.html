Past events

Each event has its own page with photos, presentations and documents.

  1. October 2025 · EFMI STC 2025, Osnabrück
    WG EDU workshop. TO VALIDATE: title and outcomes
  2. 20 May 2025 · MIE 2025, Glasgow
    WG meeting on the evolution of BMHI education, best practices and AI in education. Members agreed to build a European BMHI Education Database with AC2, to widen community engagement and to update national course data.
  3. November 2024 · EFMI STC 2024, Timișoara
    Session on the IMIA Recommendations on Education in BMHI.
  4. August 2024 · MIE 2024, Athens
    Session on the IMIA Recommendations on Education in BMHI, continuing the MEDINFO 2023 work.
  5. July 2023 · MEDINFO 2023, Sydney
    Panel on using the IMIA Recommendations in education.
  6. May 2023 · MIE 2023, Gothenburg
    Panel and workshop on the IMIA Recommendations.
  7. 29 October 2022 · Athens
    International symposium on achievements, milestones and challenges in BMHI, convened by John Mantas.
  8. September 2022 · EFMI STC 2022, Cardiff
    Educational plenary panel session.
