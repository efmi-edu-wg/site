What not to do with GenAI: a self-check for teachers

Private by design. The questionnaire runs only in your browser. Your answers are not saved, not sent anywhere and disappear when you leave or reload the page.

The three safer alternatives, explained
Local LLM
An open-weight model (for example Mistral, Llama, Qwen or Gemma) running on your own computer with tools such as Ollama, LM Studio or GPT4All. Best when the data must not leave your device.
Institutional LLM
A service your university runs or has contracted, with a data processing agreement and clear rules on retention and training. Ask your IT department or Data Protection Officer.
European LLM
A provider established in the EU that hosts data in the EU under the GDPR, for example Mistral AI, or open European models such as EuroLLM and the OpenEuroLLM project. TO VALIDATE: list of recommended providers
