Share a resource

Have you made teaching material on or with generative AI that others could reuse? Send us the link.

What we look for

  • Material used in biomedical and health informatics education, at any level.
  • A public link (for example Zenodo, GitHub or your institution's repository).
  • An open licence, such as CC BY, so others may reuse it.
  • No personal or patient data.

What happens next

  1. Your submission is stored in our database in the EU (Baserow, Germany).
  2. A WG member reviews it and may contact you if you left an e-mail.
  3. Once approved, it appears on this page with credit to the authors. Nothing is published without human approval.
