IMIA – global

International Medical Informatics Association

Health and Medical Informatics Education Working Group (WG05)

The IMIA education group. Its aims are close to ours: exchange information on BMHI programmes and courses, keep a database of education programmes, produce the international recommendations, and support the exchange of students and teachers.

Current leadership TO VALIDATE – the public page lists terms that have ended and no activity after 2019.

IMIA WG05 page

Reference document

IMIA Recommendations on Education in BMHI

The second revision (2023) describes what BMHI users, generalists and specialists need to learn in six domain areas. It is the reference for EFMI and IMIA accreditation.

IMIA regions

IMIA has six regional members. Each card shows whether we found an education group in that region.

Europe · EFMI

EFMI WG EDU

That is us. For the education groups of the European national societies, see National societies.

Education group

North America · IMIA North America

AMIA Education Working Group

A community of informatics educators and learners working on competencies, teaching practice, assessment and mentoring, from degree programmes to lifelong learning. Open to AMIA members.

Education group AMIA Education WG

Africa · HELINA

HELINA Education Working Group

Supports capacity building in health informatics in Africa. It worked on the minimum standards that let students transfer credits between MSc Health Informatics programmes in East Africa, and contributed to the Africa CDC capacity-building strategy.

Education group HELINA report 2023 (Yearbook of Medical Informatics)
