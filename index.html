Our mission

Introducing eHealth applications and the discipline of Biomedical and Health Informatics (BMHI) into healthcare requires well-educated and trained professionals. Education is the most effective way to improve how health professionals accept and use digital health. Education was the first working group EFMI established, and its members have helped update the IMIA Recommendations on Education in BMHI.

Map programmes

Share information on BMHI programmes and courses in Europe through an open database that continues the former WG/AC2 database.

Exchange practice

Exchange experiences of how BMHI is taught, and support the exchange of students and teachers between EFMI countries.

Recommend & accredit

Contribute to international recommendations and promote accreditation and certification of BMHI programmes, with EFMI AC2 and IMIA.

Teach with AI, responsibly

Give teachers practical guidance on generative AI that respects students' data and European values. Visit the GenAI Lab.
