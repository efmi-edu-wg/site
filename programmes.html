Certified and accredited courses

Programmes that hold, or have held, an accreditation by EFMI or IMIA. EFMI accreditation is granted by the EFMI Accreditation and Certification Committee (AC2) after a self-assessment and a site visit, for three years, then a re-accreditation is needed.

Open data

The database is published as open data under CC BY 4.0 (catalogue data, no personal data). Every year we deposit a versioned snapshot on Zenodo with a DOI, so it can be cited and reused in research.

Records follow the FAIR principles: they use European identifiers (country codes, EQF levels, ECTS) and are described with schema.org terms.
