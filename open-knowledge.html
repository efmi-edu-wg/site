Open science infrastructures

European open science infrastructures and how WG EDU uses them
InfrastructureWhat it isHow the WG uses it
ZenodoFree repository run by CERN and funded by the EU (OpenAIRE). Gives every deposit a DOI.Yearly snapshots of the programme database, slides, course templates, accepted manuscripts.
OpenAIREEU network that links publications, data, software and projects. OpenAIRE Explore searches all of them.Finding open outputs of EU education projects; making our outputs visible.
EOSCEuropean Open Science Cloud: a federation of research data and services.Discovering datasets and services for teaching with real-world data.
ORCIDPersistent identifier for researchers.Linking members to their publications in the member list.
Open Research EuropeThe European Commission's open access publishing platform for Horizon Europe results.Publishing results of funded projects at no cost to authors.
data.europa.euThe official portal for European open data.Open datasets for student exercises (health, population, digital skills).
Europe PMCOpen biomedical literature database, part of the European life-science infrastructure.Reading lists and literature search exercises.

Open educational resources

DOAB & DOAJ

Directories of open access books and journals, many on health informatics.

doabooks.org · doaj.org

CIDHR

Catalogue and index of digital health teaching resources, with a multilingual ontology.

About CIDHR

Synthetic data

Fictional patient records for safe hands-on teaching, e.g. Synthea.

Synthea

Health data standards

Openly available specifications to teach interoperability: HL7 FHIR, openEHR, SNOMED CT (licence via member countries).

hl7.org/fhir · openehr.org

EU funding for digital health education

Programmes that fund education, training and open resources in digital health. Check the EU Funding & Tenders Portal for open calls. TO VALIDATE: current calls

Erasmus+

Cooperation partnerships, Erasmus Mundus joint master's degrees, blended intensive programmes (short summer schools) and staff mobility.

erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu

Digital Europe Programme

Funds advanced digital skills, including master's programmes and short courses in AI, data and cybersecurity.

Digital Europe

EU4Health

Funds training of the health workforce in digital skills, linked to the European Health Data Space.

EU4Health

Horizon Europe

Research and innovation. Open access to publications is mandatory, so results become open knowledge.

Horizon Europe

COST Actions

Networking funding for researchers across Europe: meetings, training schools and short-term scientific missions.

cost.eu

EU policies that shape BMHI education

European Health Data Space

The EHDS regulation creates rights to access health data and rules for secondary use, so health professionals need new data skills.

EHDS

AI Act – AI literacy

Organisations using AI must make sure their staff have sufficient AI literacy (Article 4).

GenAI Lab

Micro-credentials & European Digital Credentials

A European approach to small, stackable courses with digitally signed certificates.

European Digital Credentials

Digital Education Action Plan

The EU plan for high-quality, inclusive digital education and digital skills.

education.ec.europa.eu
