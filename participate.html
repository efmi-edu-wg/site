How to participate

1. Tell us who you are

Fill in the short form below. We only ask for what we need to contact you (see the privacy notice).

2. Meet us

Join a WG EDU workshop at MIE or STC, or one of our online meetings.

3. Contribute

Add your programme to the database, suggest a GenAI resource, or co-author a paper.
